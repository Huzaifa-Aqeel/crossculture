## Geography

FR v1.7 describes the United States as the intended first **target market**. That is the market chosen for search planning (`regionCode=US`, English relevance), not proof of the operator's residence or the audience's location. `channels.list` country is a channel metadata field; an absent field does not prove a non-US creator, and a US field does not establish US viewers. The live-check script currently has no test-only US-country exclusion, and production screening excluded organizations but did not reject non-US/unknown channel country. The worker did, however, turn non-US/unknown channel country into an `market_unverified` scorer review flag and call it “US market presence.” This conflated separate facts. It now records `channelCountry` and `audienceGeographyStatus=unknown`, retains target-market audience geography as an unknown for all creators, and does not derive score eligibility from channel country. The US-only query/planner defaults remain the current first-market implementation; a user-selectable target-market input is not yet wired through this creator path.
