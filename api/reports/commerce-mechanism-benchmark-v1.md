# Balanced product-mechanism benchmark v1 — 21 October 2026

This is a **fixed pair-level evaluation set**, not a measurement of whether Qloo discovers the best partners. It has eight source-linked cases: one plausible and one weak mechanism in each of Outdoor & Technical, Home & Kitchen, Apparel & Lifestyle, and CPG / Personal Care. It includes Bundle and Cross-Sell objectives. The labels ask whether the exact products have distinct, evidenced roles and a concrete incremental reason to combine them under the stated objective. They do not assert cultural fit, current exact-variant availability, partner consent, resale rights, or a shared checkout. Seven cases were selected after observing app runs, so this is a regression set rather than an untouched holdout.

The fixture is [`api/fixtures/commerce-mechanism-benchmark-v1.json`](../fixtures/commerce-mechanism-benchmark-v1.json). Its product names, official public listing links, and manual first-review rationales are deliberately minimal; it stores no Shopify Catalog search result, payload, image, price, availability, or variant identifier. The first reviewer knew the app's prior outcomes, so these labels are not blinded ground truth. Seven cases came from previous signed-in native discovery. For the eighth, a read-only live replay through the shared pair-research function verified Cold Picnic and Brooklinen merchant storefronts and assessed the exact **Embrace Bathmat - Blue × Super-Plush Turkish Cotton Bath Towels** URL paths. The mechanism gate was unknown; the full opportunity was Needs Verification because the replay supplied no Qloo relationship evidence. The first attempted query pair did not return the originally proposed towel bundle, so that bundle was **not** counted. This is a focused mechanism replay, not an end-to-end native discovery result.

## Measurement protocol

1. Freeze the eight cases and the objective for each before rerunning or tuning the scorer. Give separate blank copies of [`commerce-mechanism-review-sheet-v1.json`](commerce-mechanism-review-sheet-v1.json) to two independent human reviewers; regenerate with `node api/scripts/check-commerce-mechanism-benchmark.mjs --v1 --review-sheet`. The form omits the engineer's provisional label and the app outcome.
2. Both reviewers independently read the exact official listing links, label `good` or `bad`, and record a reason or uncertainty. Resolve disagreements in writing, then fill `humanReviewA`, `humanReviewB`, and `adjudicatedLabel`. Do not equate an unknown commercial offer with a bad product mechanism.
3. Run the **same exact pair** through the app's verified merchant, exact product and variant research path, recording the mechanism gate separately from the final Supported / Needs Verification / Unsupported status. A different towel bundle or bathmat does not complete the home case. If a future rerun cannot reach the exact pair, report coverage separately rather than substituting a product.
4. `node api/scripts/check-commerce-mechanism-benchmark.mjs --v1` validates category/label balance and reports readiness. It treats legacy `mechanismPass: false` as **Unknown**, since those observations did not establish a contradiction. It withholds adjudicated mode metrics until independent human review and written adjudication. `--exploratory` shows provisional disagreements and abstentions by native versus focused mode, not measured accuracy.

Current frozen gate: **8 balanced provisional labels; 3 Supported mechanisms and 5 Unknown mechanisms (7 native discovery, 1 focused replay); 0 reviews from either human reviewer; 0 adjudications.** An Unknown mechanism is not a confirmed bad pairing or false negative. The Lacausa leggings + BAGGU handbag Bundle is a provisional-label disagreement that needs review. Later inspection-only focused replays of oil + skillet and bathmat + towels are regression checks after a general wording repair; they do not rewrite the frozen v1 observations. The apparel Cross-Sell positive is subject to independent review about whether the carry role is specific enough. The set is balanced by category and label, **not by objective**: seven Bundles and one Cross-Sell. The v2 expansion balances objectives.

Even when all eight cases are human-adjudicated, report directional coverage and conditional outcomes by execution mode. Unknown results remain abstentions. Eight curated cases give a useful regression signal, not a population-level startup quality claim. Discovery quality still needs the scoring authority's separate equal-budget Qloo-led versus category-only comparison, Precision@3 among Supported opportunities, repeatability checks, and independent human shortlist review.
