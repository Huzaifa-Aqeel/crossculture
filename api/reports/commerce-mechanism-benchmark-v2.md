# Product-mechanism benchmark v2 — 21 October 2026

This is a **fixed pair-level regression set**, not a measure of Qloo partner discovery or overall opportunity quality. The [fixture](../fixtures/commerce-mechanism-benchmark-v2.json) has 16 source-linked cases. Each of four categories contains one proposed good and one proposed bad Product Bundle, and one proposed good and one proposed bad Cross-Sell. It stores public listing URLs, a manual first-review rationale, and a small decision summary; it stores no Shopify Catalog payload, image, price, availability, or variant identifier.

The eight earlier cases came from v1; seven were chosen after seeing app runs. The eight new cases and first labels were frozen before the v2 live replay, but one engineer selected and labeled them. This is not an untouched random sample or independent human ground truth. The first label judges whether the **specified products** have distinct sourced roles and a concrete incremental benefit for the objective. It does not establish a Qloo relationship, current market eligibility, brand consent, or a shared checkout.

## Live replay and coverage

The first v2 replay used the ordinary bounded pair sampler. It assessed only the Great Jones cake pan × Brightland oil exact pair; it missed the other seven new exact pairs. A separate read-only Catalog check found **both exact product paths in the search results for all seven missed cases**. Thus the observed miss was the sampler's candidate selection, not absence from Catalog search. This is a discovery-recall defect to evaluate separately.

For a fixed-pair mechanism test, the second replay filtered each fresh, transient merchant search result to its prelisted exact product path before running the existing detail, proposal, rubric, and assessment code. It did not inject product facts, cultural evidence, or a favorable verdict. This targeted replay is deliberately **not** evidence that normal app discovery found those pairs. Exact product URL paths were compared after assessment; no alternate product was substituted.

"Supported" and "Unknown" in the table refer only to the **product-mechanism gate**. All focused replays had a final **Needs Verification** opportunity verdict because they deliberately supplied no Qloo cultural relationship. The frozen observations contain 14 assessed pairs: **6 Supported, 8 Unknown, 0 Contradicted**. Two exact pairs were unavailable. The earlier binary `mechanismPass: false` conflated Unknown with a negative decision; it has been corrected to an explicit `mechanismState` with a null legacy pass flag. The checker now reports coverage and provisional disagreements separately for native runs and focused replays. It does not count Unknown as a true negative or false negative, and it does not combine these modes into one accuracy number. One v1 apparel positive is itself subject to independent review.

The variant-divergent Lacausa × ToeSox case cannot be reduced to one product-level answer without specifying a variant. The Cocokind × Nécessaire case had both product paths in search, but exact detail failed with `selected_variant_or_product_unverified` before any pair was assessed. Neither gap is counted as a negative mechanism decision. Provider rate limits and transport failures must likewise remain separate from product judgments.

## Review gate

The [blind review sheet](commerce-mechanism-review-sheet-v2.json) contains no provisional label or app outcome. Give separate blank copies to **two independent human reviewers**. Each should inspect the official public listing links, record evidence sufficiency and exact-variant verification, then label the mechanism `good`, `bad`, or uncertain with a reason. The fixture's `reviewerA` is an engineer's provisional label and does **not** count as a human review. Enter the new labels as `humanReviewA` and `humanReviewB`, then resolve disagreements in writing and fill `adjudicatedLabel` and `adjudicationNotes`. `node api/scripts/check-commerce-mechanism-benchmark.mjs` validates balance and **withholds adjudicated mode metrics** until the human reviews and adjudications exist. `--exploratory` shows only provisional-label debugging counts. Current frozen state: **0/16 first human reviews, 0/16 second human reviews, 0/16 adjudications, 6/16 directional mechanism decisions**.

## Audit repair and inspection-only replays

The live replay tool now supports `--inspect-only --retry-exact --case=ID --trace-file=/tmp/trace.json`. This uses the exact public product paths and writes no changed observation or reviewer label to the frozen fixture. The optional developer trace contains selected product/variant/seller references, source **field kinds**, proposed decision rationale, rubric booleans, gate states, and reason codes. It contains no Catalog search/detail payload, quoted passage, image, price, or availability. The trace is opt-in and should remain in a temporary developer location. It cannot make a focused replay count as native discovery.

Inspection-only replays found two concrete wording failures: a verified oil-plus-skillet Bundle was marked Unknown when its benefit paraphrased the roles, and a bathmat-plus-towels Bundle was marked Unknown when an otherwise concrete benefit contained the ordinary word “together.” The redundant exact-role repetition gate and broad benefit word veto were removed; the isolated rubric and objective challenge still reject generic co-use. Both exact pairs reached a Supported **mechanism** in subsequent focused replays, while their full opportunity verdict stayed Needs Verification because no Qloo relationship was supplied. These are regression observations, not independent accuracy results.

The two-olive-oil Cross-Sell replay showed distinct sourced finishing and cooking roles; its provisional Bad label is therefore disputed, not a reason to ban same-category items. The two-pack Cross-Sell still passed a new general incremental-need challenge with a claimed full-day carry versus compact errand use; whether that is commercially persuasive needs independent human review. A targeted exact merchant-page retry is now available only when selected-item facts or role grounding are missing. It is bounded to the deficient side of the exact listing and does not turn absent evidence into a negative decision.

Do not use this same set both to tune the duplicate-function rubric and to claim independent validation. After a general fix, rerun it as regression coverage and collect a fresh, untouched holdout. End-to-end recommendation quality still needs the scoring authority's equal-budget Qloo-led versus category-only baseline, independent shortlist review, and repeatability checks. The current fixture is a useful fault-finding instrument; it cannot justify a startup-level accuracy claim.
