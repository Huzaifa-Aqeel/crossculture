## Verified rule behavior and correction

- R and G are computed from separate frozen fields. An offline scorer regression now confirms that a source-backed, bridge-linked partner activity can produce R=.50 while G remains null if promotional capabilities are missing. The actual BAGGU packets have no admitted `partnerActivity`, so this distinction does not retroactively score them.
- Existing promotion fixtures confirm a hypothetical joint campaign/story can qualify from independently demonstrated channels and partner creation activity, with no prior collaboration or agreement. They reject a speculative future campaign, unsupported channel or unrelated page.
- A failing regression reproduced the Cold Picnic navigation false positive before the matcher stopped counting bare “Partners” as a business action. The worker's capability claim now says relevance to the joint route is unverified whenever the promotion gate does not admit that capability. A second failing regression reproduced Lacausa's legal-notice Extract selection; non-evidence URLs are now skipped before selecting the next official lead.
- Existing snapshots were not rewritten. The cached source excerpts are short contextual projections, not full pages; they cannot establish facts absent from the captured text. More targeted future research may find valid evidence, but it must pass the same Qloo, attribution, capability, freeze and v4 gates.

The three null assessments are therefore justified by their **admitted frozen evidence**, while Cold Picnic's capability claim and Lacausa's wasted Extract path were real pipeline defects. No new scored recommendation is asserted from this offline audit.
